01 / PROJECT INFORMATION

Insight, with understanding.

We are developing a web-based AI investment assistant that brings financial news, risk analysis, prediction-market insights, and financial education into one platform.

Beginners can learn financial concepts through guided explanations, while experienced users can explore connected analytical tools. Across the platform, outputs will show their supporting sources and explain how insights are generated.

04 / METHODS AND RESULTS

Behind the insights.

01Financial news and sentiment analysisFrom individual articles to asset-level trends.+

Financial news will be collected through Finnhub, with historical OHLCV price data from yfinance. Articles will be cleaned and deduplicated before analysis.

Jev AI System One will classify sentiment as positive, negative, or neutral and assign scores from −1 to +1 with confidence information. A generative language model will produce validated one-sentence summaries of no more than 280 characters. Daily indicators will combine confidence and publisher-authority weights, with three-day and seven-day exponential moving averages.

Interactive charts will compare sentiment with price movements without implying direct causation.

Evaluation plan

Data retrieval reliability, summary-format compliance, sentiment validity, processing latency, trend-calculation accuracy, and dashboard usability.

02Risk-contagion analysisMaking structural connections visible.+

A directed, heterogeneous Neo4j knowledge graph will represent more than 100 Hong Kong and U.S. listed companies and their relationships. Evidence will come from HKEXnews disclosures, SEC filings, and annual reports, with LEI/CIK identifiers and fuzzy name matching for entity resolution.

Three channels will be modelled: equity ownership, supply-chain dependencies, and shared board memberships. Inverse transmission-intensity weights will support Dijkstra path finding, while multiplicative attenuation will estimate shock propagation. Queries will be limited to four hops, with a target response time below 200 ms.

An interactive network will accompany step-by-step explanations linked to supporting evidence.

Evaluation plan

Planned checks: relationship-data quality, path-calculation correctness, query performance, and evidence support and clarity of explanations.

03Prediction-market insightsUnderstanding changes in collective expectations.+

The initial scope will cover U.S. monetary policy and related macroeconomic events. Polymarket will provide the primary data; equivalent Kalshi contracts will support comparisons where event definitions and resolution conditions match.

Binary YES prices will serve as approximate market-implied probabilities. The module will track one-hour, six-hour, and 24-hour changes, considering trading volume, liquidity, bid-ask spreads, and order-book depth when interpreting significant movements.

Retrieved news and movements in Treasury yields, equity indices, foreign exchange, and commodities will provide context. Probability histories, a connected-event graph, and a radar chart will distinguish potential associations from established causal relationships.

Evaluation plan

Retrieval accuracy, consistency across comparable contracts, reliability of change detection, factual support for explanations, and visualisation usability.

04Financial interpretation chatbotFinancial explanations grounded in retrieved evidence.+

The chatbot will draw on curated investor-education materials, platform-method documentation, internal module outputs, and on-demand web search. Documents will retain source, topic, jurisdiction, date, and version metadata.

Hybrid retrieval will combine BM25 keyword search and dense-vector search, merged through reciprocal rank fusion with optional reranking. An optional profile will tailor explanations to knowledge, goals, investment horizon, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and disclosed holdings. Profile data will have user-specific access controls and deletion options.

A manually reviewed question set will compare plain LLM, dense-only RAG, and hybrid RAG configurations. Web search will be evaluated separately.

Evaluation plan

Retrieval relevance, factual accuracy, citation support, clarity, latency, and cost.
